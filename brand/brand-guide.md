# AlamakLah brand guide

## Tone
- Malaysian, conversational, like a friend explaining something at the mamak.
- Funny, curious, slightly cheeky. Never mean-spirited.
- Not corporate, not generic AI ("In today's fast-paced world..." is banned).
- English-first narration. Malaysian flavour (lah, alamak, walao) allowed sparingly, where it lands naturally.

## Visual style: blocky 3D
- Characters built from primitives: box torso, blocky head, simple limbs, a face with eyes and mouth.
- Flat pastel colours.
- Soft lighting with visible shadows.
- Perspective camera.
- Real 3D only: depth, lighting, shadows, camera movement in 3D space. No pile of 2D shapes.
- Reusable components: Character, Scene, Caption, TimeLabel, CounterCard.
- Animations: idle bob, walk, head tilt, shock shake, point/arm raise, pop-in props, camera push-in/pull-out/pan.
